Common Questions

Asked & Answered

Everything you need to know before you place your order.

Start Shopping
Is the jewellery real gold or gold-plated?
+

Our jewellery is crafted from premium stainless steel with gold, rose-gold, or silver plating. It's hypoallergenic, skin-safe, and designed to maintain its luster for years with proper care.

How long does shipping take?
+

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days across India. Express options are available at checkout. All orders above ₹599 receive free insured shipping.

Can I return or exchange if it doesn't fit?
+

Absolutely. We offer a 30-day return policy with no questions asked. Contact our team and we'll arrange a free pickup and full refund or exchange.

Do you offer bridal set customisation?
+

Yes! We offer personalised bridal consultations where we tailor complete sets to your outfit, skin tone, and occasion. Contact us to book a free session.

Is it safe to wear daily?
+

Yes. Our stainless steel base is hypoallergenic and skin-safe for sensitive skin. Avoid contact with perfume and water for maximum longevity.
